BIO

Born in the concrete jungles of Surulere, Lagos, PayBac Iboro delivers a potent blend of Nigerian Hip Hop, African Melodies and graphic story telling. PayBac first gained National recognition, with his second album “CULT!”, which earned Iboro his second Headies nomination after previously being nominated for his work on Alternate Ending with Boogey. Inspired by the greats from Fela Kuti and William Onyeabor to Jay Z and Kendrick Lamar, PayBac Iboro’s sound is defined by eclectic drums, with lyricism in a Nigerian accent, and lyrics that explore the life of an average Nigerian told from a very unique perspective.
